# FlexPagina pricing

Current pricing is defined in `lib/pricing.ts`, with monthly EUR amounts excluding VAT.

| Plan | Price | Published pages/site | Analytics |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Bronze | 7.95 | 3 | Visitors and pageviews, date filters |
| Silver | 14.95 | 10 | Popular pages, sources, devices, 30/90-day trends |
| Gold | 24.95 | Unlimited (fair-use) | Conversions, monthly dashboard report, up to 12 months |

All plans retain core section types, unlimited sections, hosting, custom-domain connection, basic SEO and social links. Draft pages/designs are unrestricted. Advanced page SEO, GA4 configuration and existing enquiry features require Silver/Gold. Gold includes multilingual and existing service/review capabilities.

Booking & Facturatie is optional on every plan at +19, including Gold; calendar/iCal and booking-to-PDF invoices remain under it. Multilingual is +2.99 on Bronze/Silver, included on Gold. Public pricing does not claim branding removal or an unverified support policy.

Stored active/trial/paid-through canceled subscriptions govern rights. Missing or inactive subscriptions have Bronze base access without add-ons; new subscriptions default to Bronze. Existing billing records and live content are preserved. Billing/purchases are placeholders and remain disabled. Legacy item prices need an explicit manual provider migration, not an automatic database rewrite.

See [pricing and analytics rollout](pricing-analytics-rollout.md) for the migration, consent/retention methodology, fair-use safeguards, safe downgrade proposal, Stripe steps and verification.
