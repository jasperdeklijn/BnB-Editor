# FlexPagina pricing

Current pricing is defined in `lib/pricing.ts`, with monthly EUR amounts excluding VAT.

**Temporary access, 13 October 2026:** all authenticated users receive Gold, Booking & Facturatie and multilingual access for free while subscriptions are unavailable. The catalog below remains the intended paid offering. Stored billing records are preserved. See [temporary Gold access](temporary-gold-access.md) for the application/database switches and coordinated rollout.

| Plan | Price | Published pages/site | Analytics |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Bronze | 7.95 | 3 | Visitors and pageviews, date filters |
| Silver | 14.95 | 10 | Popular pages, sources, devices, 30/90-day trends |
| Gold | 24.95 | Unlimited (fair-use) | Conversions, monthly dashboard report, up to 12 months |

All plans retain core section types, unlimited sections, hosting, custom-domain connection, basic SEO and social links. Draft pages/designs are unrestricted. Advanced page SEO, GA4 configuration and existing enquiry features require Silver/Gold. Gold includes multilingual and existing service/review capabilities.

The current catalog lists Booking & Facturatie at +14.95 on every plan, including Gold; calendar/iCal and booking-to-PDF invoices remain under it. Multilingual is +2.99 on Bronze/Silver, included on Gold. Both are temporarily free under the access policy above. Public pricing does not claim branding removal or an unverified support policy.

When temporary access is disabled in both the application and database, stored active/trial/paid-through canceled subscriptions govern rights. Missing or inactive subscriptions then have Bronze base access without add-ons; new subscription records default to Bronze. Existing billing records and live content are preserved. Billing/purchases are placeholders and remain disabled. Legacy item prices need an explicit manual provider migration, not an automatic database rewrite.

See [pricing and analytics rollout](pricing-analytics-rollout.md) for the migration, consent/retention methodology, fair-use safeguards, safe downgrade proposal, Stripe steps and verification.
