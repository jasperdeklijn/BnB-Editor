# Subscription Plans

## Overview

The website builder offers three subscription tiers that scale with the needs of a business.

The philosophy behind the pricing is simple:

- **Bronze** → Get your business online.
- **Silver** → Receive customer inquiries.
- **Gold** → Let customers book appointments online.

This creates a natural upgrade path instead of only limiting the number of sections.
